2. **Concrete place, time, sensation** — the orchard behind the house, shoulders, the highest apples: place and body, not abstractions.
3. **Sounds truly happened** — the small honest detail is the weight: "I was too heavy, and we both knew it, and neither of us said so." No greeting-card language anywhere.
4. **Single clean sentence, every word earns its place** — 205 characters, one sentence, no filler; the repeated "and" carries the quiet accumulation of the moment.
5. **Warm ache that lingers** — the sentence ends on a silence shared between father and child; the ache is the growing up itself, unstated.
